Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a vendor?+
Browse vendors, view their profile, choose a service or package, then click "Request Booking." You'll receive instant confirmation.
Are all vendors verified?+
Yes! Every single vendor on Epic Hub has been through our verification process — ID, business review, and quality screening — before being listed.
Can I cancel a booking?+
Cancellations accepted up to 7 days before the event for a full refund. Within 7 days is subject to the vendor's policy.
What payment methods are accepted?+
We accept Fawry, Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, and credit/debit cards via Paymob.
Can I plan multiple events at once?+
Absolutely! Your dashboard supports multiple events simultaneously, each with its own vendors, budget tracker, and checklist.
How does pricing work?+
Vendors set their own prices and you see them upfront — no hidden fees. You can also message vendors for custom quotes before committing.
Is Epic Hub available outside Cairo?+
Yes! We have vendors in Cairo, New Cairo, Heliopolis, Zamalek, Giza, Alexandria, and more cities across Egypt — with new cities launching soon.
How do I become a vendor?+
Click "Become a Vendor" in the navigation. Fill in your details, upload portfolio samples, and our team will review and approve your profile within 24 hours.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as name, email, phone number, and event details when you register or make a booking. We also collect usage data to improve our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to connect you with vendors, process bookings, send notifications, and improve Epic Hub's services. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We share only the information necessary with vendors to fulfill your bookings (name, phone, event details). Vendors agree to our data protection terms and may not use your data for any other purpose.

4. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@epichub.eg. We will respond within 72 hours.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@epichub.eg or +20 100 000 0000.

Last updated: March 1, 2026

By using Epic Hub, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use our platform.

2. Platform Use

Epic Hub is a marketplace connecting event clients with vendors. We facilitate connections and provide booking tools but are not a party to vendor-client contracts.

3. Bookings & Payments

Bookings are confirmed once a vendor accepts your request. Payment terms are set per booking. Cancellation policies vary by vendor and are displayed on their profile.

4. Vendor Responsibility

Vendors are independent service providers. Epic Hub verifies credentials but does not guarantee service quality beyond our vetting process. Disputes are handled through our support team.
