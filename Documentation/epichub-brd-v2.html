01

Business Problem & Opportunity

The business case for EpicHub is grounded in a structural market failure — not a product gap. Understanding the problem precisely is what determines whether the business has a defensible reason to exist.

The Core Market Failure

Planning an event in Egypt requires customers to individually identify, contact, negotiate, and pay multiple vendors through entirely disconnected channels — phone calls, Instagram DMs, and bank transfers. There is no trust infrastructure, no price transparency, and no accountability layer. Vendors, in turn, have no scalable digital sales channel and depend entirely on word-of-mouth. Both sides lose time, money, and confidence in every transaction.

Problem Dimensions

⚠  Customer-Side Problems
  • 🔍No centralized discovery — must search across Instagram, WhatsApp groups, and recommendations individually
  • 💸No price transparency — vendors quote based on perceived customer budget, not a published rate
  • 🤔No trust signals — impossible to verify vendor quality, reliability, or business legitimacy before paying
  • 📱No booking confirmation — verbal or WhatsApp agreements with no formal record or payment protection
  • ⏱️Extremely time-consuming — sourcing 5 vendors for one event can take weeks of back-and-forth
⚠  Vendor-Side Problems
  • 📢No scalable marketing channel — entirely dependent on word-of-mouth and personal social media presence
  • 💰Inconsistent revenue — booking pipeline is unpredictable with no visibility into demand
  • 🧾Payment collection risk — customers sometimes cancel or dispute after service delivery with no protection
  • 📊No performance data — no way to measure conversion, compare pricing, or understand market demand
  • 🏆No verified credibility — quality vendors cannot differentiate themselves from low-quality competitors online

Market Opportunity

Egypt's event services market is large, informal, and growing. Corporate events represent 59–60% of event spend across the MENA region. The digital transaction rate for event services in Egypt is near zero — every booking is done offline. No existing MENA platform offers end-to-end booking for all event types with verified vendors and secure payment. EpicHub enters an uncontested space in Egypt with a model that works at global scale.

Business Objectives

Nine measurable objectives that define what EpicHub must achieve. Every business requirement in Section 04 traces to at least one of these objectives. Changes to requirements must be evaluated against objective impact.

BO-01

Market Leadership in Egypt

Establish EpicHub as the leading event vendor marketplace in Egypt within 18 months of launch, measured by booking volume and brand awareness among target segments.

BO-02

Positive Unit Economics

Achieve positive unit economics within Year 1 — meaning revenue per booking exceeds variable cost per booking — without dependence on any single revenue stream.

BO-03

Vendor Network Critical Mass

Onboard sufficient verified vendors across all core service categories so that no customer search in Egypt returns zero results in any of the 5 primary categories.

BO-04

Build a Trusted Brand

Establish EpicHub as synonymous with safe, verified event booking in Egypt — measured by average platform rating ≥ 4.5 stars and a dispute rate below 3% of bookings.

BO-05

Maximize Customer Lifetime Value

Drive repeat usage through loyalty, promotions, and service quality — targeting a 35% repeat booking rate within the first year of a customer's account lifetime.

BO-06

Corporate Revenue Penetration

Secure a minimum of 20 active enterprise corporate accounts within 12 months, contributing a defined percentage of total platform GMV and anchoring B2B revenue.

BO-07

Operational Efficiency via AI

Resolve 80% of customer support inquiries via AI chatbot without human escalation — reducing cost per support interaction and enabling the ops team to scale without linear headcount growth.

BO-08

Vendor Revenue Growth

Demonstrate to vendors that the platform increases their bookings — a retention metric measured by vendor churn below 5% per quarter and subscription tier upgrade rates.

BO-09

MENA Expansion Readiness

Complete all business setup (legal entity, payment partnerships, regulatory compliance) required to launch in a second MENA market by the end of Phase 1 — ready to execute in Phase 2.

05

Business Rules

Business rules are operational policies that govern how the EpicHub marketplace works. They are non-negotiable unless a formal change request is approved against the relevant business objective. These rules exist to protect platform integrity, trust, and legal standing.

BR-001
Vendor Trust

No vendor profile may appear in customer-facing search results until explicitly approved by EpicHub operations. All content — text, images, pricing — is owned and curated by EpicHub. Vendors submit content for review; they do not publish it directly.

BR-002
Escrow

Customer payments are held in escrow from booking confirmation until either: (a) the customer confirms delivery, or (b) 48 hours elapse after the event date with no dispute raised. Vendor receives payout within 72 hours of the release trigger. EpicHub deducts its commission before disbursement.

BR-003
Reviews

A customer may only submit one review per booking, and only after the booking status is Delivered. Reviews cannot be submitted more than 30 days after the event date. Reviews are moderated by EpicHub before publication. No anonymous reviews are permitted.

BR-004
Cancellation

Free cancellation is available up to 7 days before the event. Cancellations within 7 days incur a 25% platform fee. Cancellations within 48 hours are non-refundable. If a vendor cancels within 7 days of the event, the customer receives a full refund plus a 10% goodwill credit. Vendor-initiated late cancellations may result in account suspension.

BR-005
Commission

EpicHub charges a platform commission on each confirmed booking, automatically deducted from the escrow payout before vendor receives funds. Commission rates vary by vendor subscription tier and are reviewed quarterly. Commission rates are commercially confidential and not displayed to customers.

BR-006
Disputes

Disputes must be raised within 48 hours of the event date. A dispute automatically freezes the relevant escrow. Each booking is eligible for one dispute only. EpicHub issues a binding resolution within 5 business days. Resolution decisions are final and logged with a full audit trail.

BR-007
Verification

A vendor must hold and provide a valid Egyptian business license to receive the EpicHub Verified badge. License validity is checked at onboarding and re-verified annually. An expired license results in immediate badge removal and profile suspension until renewed.

BR-008
Loyalty

Loyalty points are credited only upon confirmed service delivery — not at booking time. Points expire 12 months from issue date. Points cannot be transferred, sold, or converted to cash. Maximum combined discount from points + promotional codes is capped at 30% of booking value per transaction.

BR-009
Corporate

Corporate accounts require a designated account administrator. Corporate bookings generate monthly consolidated invoices in Egyptian legal format including VAT. Corporate account holders are not eligible to use personal loyalty points. Corporate accounts may negotiate custom commission structures via enterprise agreement.

BR-010
Promotions

Only one promotional discount code may be applied per booking. Promotional campaigns are created, activated, and deactivated exclusively by EpicHub operations — vendors may not create their own promotions independently on the platform.

BR-011
Data Retention

Financial records (bookings, payments, invoices) must be retained for a minimum of 5 years for Egyptian tax and audit compliance. Customer personal data may be deleted on valid request, subject to mandatory financial retention obligations. Data handling must comply with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.

BR-012
Quality

Any vendor maintaining an average rating below 3.5 stars over a rolling 90-day period enters a formal quality review. Vendors below 3.0 stars are suspended from appearing in new search results pending remediation. Persistent low ratings result in platform removal with 30 days' notice.

07

Revenue Model

EpicHub's revenue model is designed for multi-stream diversification — no single stream should dominate. The model monetizes both sides of the marketplace and evolves with the platform's maturity.

💸
Transactional

Booking Commission

Percentage fee on each confirmed booking, deducted automatically from vendor payout at escrow release. Rate varies by subscription tier. This is the primary revenue engine at launch.

📦
Recurring

Vendor Subscriptions

Basic / Standard / Premium monthly subscription tiers for vendors. Higher tiers unlock better search ranking, analytics access, and featured placement. Recurring revenue baseline.

⚡
Transactional

Customer Service Fee

Small, transparent service fee added to customer checkout total. Disclosed before payment confirmation. Covers platform transaction costs and customer protection infrastructure.

👑
Recurring

Premium Customer Features

Paid customer tier unlocking priority booking access, early deal notifications, expanded shortlist limits, and enhanced loyalty multipliers. Secondary revenue stream.

🏛️
Contracted

Corporate Enterprise Plans

Custom enterprise agreements for corporate accounts — negotiated pricing, volume discounts, dedicated account management, and consolidated billing. High-value, high-retention contracts.

🎪
Phase 2+

Direct Event Execution

EpicHub produces fully managed events as a vendor, leveraging the platform's curated network. Higher margin per transaction. Transforms EpicHub from marketplace to full-service event company.

Revenue Diversification Principle

No single revenue stream should exceed 60% of total platform revenue in Year 2. Commission will dominate Year 1 — the business goal is to grow subscriptions and enterprise plans to reduce transactional revenue dependency before scaling marketing spend.

Risk Register

Business risks — not technical risks (those belong in the PRD). These are threats to the business model, commercial viability, or market position of EpicHub.

R-01

Vendor Supply Shortage at Launch

Insufficient verified vendors means poor search results and customer abandonment before the platform gets traction. The marketplace has no value without supply. Mitigation: Pre-launch vendor acquisition campaign; category-minimum thresholds as a launch gate.

High Prob
High Impact
R-02

Low Consumer Trust in Online Payment for Services

Egyptian customers are accustomed to paying cash on delivery for services. Convincing customers to pay online before receiving a service is a behavioral change the business must drive. Mitigation: Prominent escrow guarantee messaging; money-back guarantee for legitimate disputes; social proof from early adopters.

High Prob
High Impact
R-03

Vendor Resistance to Commission Model

Vendors may reject the commission model and continue directing customers to off-platform transactions to avoid fees. This undermines the revenue model and escrow protection. Mitigation: Strict terms prohibiting off-platform solicitation; demonstrate lead generation value in first 90 days; reduced commission introductory period.

Med Prob
High Impact
R-04

Regulatory or Payment Compliance Gaps

Egyptian e-commerce payment regulations or escrow licensing requirements may not be fully met at launch, creating legal exposure or forcing a payment model change. Mitigation: Legal review pre-launch; CBE guidance consultation; Paymob contractual alignment on escrow structure.

Low Prob
High Impact
R-05

Competitor Replication

A well-funded competitor (local or regional) copies the model and launches in Egypt during EpicHub's growth phase with a larger marketing budget. Mitigation: Speed of execution; deep vendor relationships; brand investment from day one; proprietary vendor data as defensible moat.

Med Prob
Med Impact
R-06

Escrow / Payment Partner Failure

Primary payment gateway (Paymob) fails, is unavailable, or revokes escrow agreement, disrupting all bookings and payouts on the platform. Mitigation: Parallel integration with Stripe as fallback; contractual SLA with payment partner; escrow held in segregated account.

Low Prob
High Impact
R-07

Vendor Quality Degradation Post-Launch

Rapid vendor onboarding to meet supply targets results in low-quality vendors joining the platform, damaging brand trust and triggering a spike in disputes and negative reviews. Mitigation: Minimum quality bar at onboarding; proactive rating monitoring; BR-012 quality review process.

Med Prob
Med Impact
R-08

Corporate Sales Cycle Too Long

Enterprise accounts take 3–6 months to close, meaning BO-06 (20 accounts in Year 1) may not be achievable without a dedicated B2B sales function from launch. Mitigation: Assign dedicated corporate sales resource from Month 1; target SMEs and mid-size companies before large enterprises.

Med Prob
Med Impact

Business Constraints & Assumptions

⚠  Business Constraints
  • 🗺️Phase 1 operates exclusively in the Egypt market. No cross-border transactions or non-Egyptian vendor onboarding in Phase 1.
  • 💳BNPL / instalment payment integrations (Tamara, tabby) are a Phase 2 commercial initiative requiring separate partnership agreements.
  • 🎪EpicHub acting as a direct event producer is a Phase 2+ strategic revenue stream. Not part of the Phase 1 business model.
  • 📱Native mobile applications are not part of the Phase 1 go-to-market — web-first approach limits acquisition to web channels only.
  • 🏢Corporate enterprise module (RFQ management, approval workflows) requires dedicated B2B sales resources not yet confirmed for Phase 1.
✓  Business Assumptions
  • ✅Sufficient vendor appetite exists in Egypt to reach a viable supply-side critical mass within 6 months of launch outreach.
  • ✅Egyptian customers are willing to pay online for event services when adequate trust signals (escrow, verified badges, reviews) are present.
  • ✅Paymob escrow agreement will be contractually available and technically implemented before the launch date.
  • ✅EpicHub operations team will be fully staffed to handle vendor review, dispute resolution, and support escalation from Day 1 of public launch.
  • ✅No material changes to Egyptian e-commerce or payment regulation will occur in the 12 months following launch.
